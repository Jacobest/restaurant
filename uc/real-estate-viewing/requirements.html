What you need to make this work

Atlantic Seaboard Properties turns listing enquiries into booked viewings on WhatsApp. Buyer contact and finance details are personal data, so POPIA applies from the first message.

Prepared 7 October 2026 for South African businesses. “Easy” means cheap or free and quick to set up. “Ideal” means the best fit as you grow. ⚠ means check with the vendor before promising it.

1. The basics for any WhatsApp AI chat

A Meta Business accountThe WhatsApp Business Platform sits under Meta. S10U is a Meta Business Partner (shown on its site).
A phone number that is not already on WhatsAppA dedicated number, ideally the number customers already know. Moving a number off the normal WhatsApp app needs care.
A business display nameFor example “Atlantic Seaboard Properties”. Meta checks it against your real brand.
Business verification (and a green tick, optional)Meta verifies the business. The green tick is Meta’s decision, not guaranteed.
Approved message templatesFree-form replies are allowed for 24 hours after the customer’s last message. Outside that window only pre-approved templates can be sent (reminders, rebooking nudges).
Opt-in and opt-outGet permission before you message first, and make stopping easy (S10U lists automatic opt-out handling).
Your content to train the botFAQs, price list, menu or service list, opening hours, policies. The bot can only answer from what you give it.
A list of systems to connectCalendar, CRM, payments and so on. The tables on this page show easy and ideal options.
A hand-over planWho answers when the bot cannot, and during which hours. S10U has an inbox with agent hand-over.

3. Connections for this demo

Each row shows the simplest option first, then the better long-term options. Where S10U’s own connectors are not confirmed, the link can usually be made through an API or an automation tool such as Make, n8n or Zapier.

Listing data

The bot confirms the property, price and details from the listing the buyer tapped.

Easy
A Google Sheet or CSV of your active listings

Free. Staff update it, or export it from your agency software. Reference, price, suburb, bedrooms and photo link are enough.

Ideal
Agency software feed: PropData, Entegral or similar

These systems already syndicate listings to the portals. Whether they expose a buyer-facing API or export to the bot must be confirmed with each vendor. ⚠

Lead source (property portals)

Buyers arrive from Property24, Private Property or your own site.

Easy
A “WhatsApp the agent” link with the listing reference pre-filled

A click-to-chat link with a ready message. Add it to your own website and social ads. Whether the portals let you add this link to portal ads must be checked. ⚠

Ideal
Property24 and Private Property lead emails into the CRM

Entegral, PropData and PropCon state that they import portal leads from email. The bot can then message the buyer. Direct portal APIs were not verified. ⚠

Check each portal’s terms before contacting a buyer who only emailed. Message first only with opt-in. ⚠

Agent diary and viewing slots

Free, with an API. Agents see the viewing on their phone.

Common in larger South African agencies.

Ideal

Easy booking pages with calendar sync. API access depends on plan. ⚠

Agent alert

The agent gets the buyer’s summary when a viewing is booked.

Easy
WhatsApp message and email to the agent

Sent by the bot with name, budget, bedrooms, bond status and time.

Ideal
Push into the CRM as a task

The agent sees it in the same place as other leads.

Directions and reminders

Send a map pin and reminders before the viewing to reduce no-shows.

Easy
Google Maps link and approved WhatsApp reminder templates

Free. S10U lists scheduled broadcasts with automatic opt-out.

Ideal
Reminders triggered by the agent’s diary

Bond pre-approval

Know if the buyer can afford the home before the agent spends time.

Easy
Bot asks “yes, not yet or cash” and the agent checks

No documents in chat. The bot only records the answer.

Ideal
Bond originator partner

Refer “not yet” buyers to a bond originator. Needs a signed referral agreement and the buyer’s consent. ⚠

Financial details are sensitive. Do not collect ID numbers or bank statements in chat. ⚠

Saved to the CRM note or a Google Sheet.

Ideal
Google Business Profile review link

Send a review link to happy buyers after the sale.

4. The AI behind the chat

AI provider

The model that writes the replies.

Easy
Gemini Flash or a “mini” tier model

Cheapest. Good for FAQs, menus and simple booking questions.

Ideal
A frontier model with strong tool use (for example Claude Sonnet or the top OpenAI model)

Better at multi-step bookings and calling your systems. Costs more per chat.

OpenAI, Anthropic and Google are reachable from South Africa and bill in US dollars. Keep the model swappable. S10U does not name its AI models on its site, so ask. ⚠

MCP (later)

A standard way for the AI to use your tools.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets an AI model use another tool’s functions, such as “read this calendar” or “create this invoice”. Several popular tools offer MCP servers (for example Zapier, HubSpot, Xero, Stripe, Shopify and Google Workspace). Confirm current status before promising it. ⚠ It is a “later” option here: most demos work with simple connections first.

5. Privacy (POPIA)

  • You are the “responsible party”. S10U, the AI provider and any automation tool are “operators” and need a written operator agreement.
  • Collect only what you need. Say why when you collect it. Keep it only as long as needed.
  • Sending data to overseas AI providers is a cross-border transfer. Tell customers, and use providers with a data processing agreement and no training on your data.
  • Health information is special personal information. Collect booking details only in chat. Send clinical questions to the practice.
  • Appoint an Information Officer and know how to report a breach to the Information Regulator.
  • This is a checklist, not legal advice. Get legal sign-off for medical clients.
