What you need to make this work

Bluegum Primary School answers fee questions, takes payments, sends circulars and logs absences on WhatsApp. Children’s and family financial data is sensitive, so the bot verifies the parent first.

Prepared 7 October 2026 for South African businesses. “Easy” means cheap or free and quick to set up. “Ideal” means the best fit as you grow. ⚠ means check with the vendor before promising it.

1. The basics for any WhatsApp AI chat

A Meta Business accountThe WhatsApp Business Platform sits under Meta. S10U is a Meta Business Partner (shown on its site).
A phone number that is not already on WhatsAppA dedicated number, ideally the number customers already know. Moving a number off the normal WhatsApp app needs care.
A business display nameFor example “Bluegum Primary School”. Meta checks it against your real brand.
Business verification (and a green tick, optional)Meta verifies the business. The green tick is Meta’s decision, not guaranteed.
Approved message templatesFree-form replies are allowed for 24 hours after the customer’s last message. Outside that window only pre-approved templates can be sent (reminders, rebooking nudges).
Opt-in and opt-outGet permission before you message first, and make stopping easy (S10U lists automatic opt-out handling).
Your content to train the botFAQs, price list, menu or service list, opening hours, policies. The bot can only answer from what you give it.
A list of systems to connectCalendar, CRM, payments and so on. The tables on this page show easy and ideal options.
A hand-over planWho answers when the bot cannot, and during which hours. S10U has an inbox with agent hand-over.

Parent verification

Confirm it is the registered parent before showing any fee or child details.

Easy
SMS or WhatsApp code to the cellphone number on the school’s record

The bot only answers for children linked to that number. Wrong code or unknown number goes to the office.

Ideal
Check against the school system’s guardian list

Match the number to the parent record automatically. Needs the school system link below. ⚠

Ideal
Extra check for sensitive requests

For example, ask for the child’s date of birth before sending a statement.

Children’s data and family finances are protected by POPIA. Never share a balance or child name until the parent is verified, and log who asked. ⚠

School administration and fee system

Where learners, parents and fee accounts live. The bot reads the balance from here.

Easy
An exported fee list (CSV or Google Sheet) updated by the bursar

Cheap and fine for a demo or a small school. The balance is only as fresh as the last export.

Ideal
D6 (d6+ and D6 Communicator)

Widely used South African school platform covering admin, finance and parent communication. Whether it offers an open API to third parties is not stated publicly. Ask D6. ⚠

Ideal
SchoolSpeak, Edupac or a Pastel-based fee system

Common in South African schools. API access was not verified for any of them, so expect a vendor partnership or an export. ⚠

Fee and child records are personal information under POPIA. Keep the data in the school system and read it live instead of copying it. ⚠

4. The AI behind the chat

AI provider

The model that writes the replies.

Easy
OpenAI GPT-4o through S10U (stated on its site)

Already part of the platform, so nothing extra to set up. Good for FAQs, menus and booking questions.

Ideal
A private or fine-tuned model (S10U lists both)

For sensitive data such as health or finance, ask S10U how the private option works, so chat text stays under your control.

S10U names OpenAI models, “multi-LLM routing” and private models. Ask whether you can bring your own key or use other providers. Sending chat text to an overseas AI is a cross-border transfer under POPIA. ⚠

MCP (later)

A standard way for the AI to use your tools.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets an AI model use another tool’s functions, such as “read this calendar” or “create this invoice”. S10U does not mention MCP. Its API, webhooks and 80+ integrations are the route to try first. Treat MCP as a later option and ask S10U whether it is planned. ⚠

5. Privacy (POPIA)

  • You are the “responsible party”. S10U, the AI provider and any automation tool are “operators” and need a written operator agreement.
  • Collect only what you need. Say why when you collect it. Keep it only as long as needed.
  • Sending data to overseas AI providers is a cross-border transfer. Tell customers, and use providers with a data processing agreement and no training on your data.
  • Health information is special personal information. Collect booking details only in chat. Send clinical questions to the practice.
  • Appoint an Information Officer and know how to report a breach to the Information Regulator.
  • This is a checklist, not legal advice. Get legal sign-off for medical clients.
