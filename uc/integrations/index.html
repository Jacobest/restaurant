Integration guide

What you need to connect, by type of tool, with an easy option and one or more ideal options for each. Chosen for what South African small businesses use most, with the information we have today.

Prepared 7 October 2026. 30 tool types. We will update this guide when S10U answers our questions. Popularity is our judgement, not market share. ⚠ means the API access, price or availability was not verified. Per-demo versions are linked under “Used in”.

Bookings and calendars

Calendars and appointment booking

Free times, booking slots and reminders.

Easy
Google Calendar

Free, with an API and push notifications. Works everywhere in South Africa. One calendar per person or room.

Ideal
Microsoft 365 / Outlook (Graph API)

Common in South African firms. Local resellers bill in rand. Works with Make and Zapier.

Ideal
Calendly

Easy booking pages with an API and Zapier. Billed in US dollars.

Ideal
Cal.com

Open source with an API and webhooks. Can be self-hosted.

Restaurant table management

Tables, covers and reservations.

Easy
Google Calendar or a Google Sheet

The bot writes the booking, staff confirm it.

Ideal
Dineplan

The South African leader, and the only local platform with Reserve with Google. Its API is read-only (GET), so the bot can read bookings but cannot create them. Keys on request, API fee about R190 a month ex VAT per account (check). ⚠

Reviews and reputation

Google reviews

Ask happy customers to review you, and answer reviews.

Easy
Google Business Profile review link or QR code

Free. Send the link in WhatsApp right after the visit.

Ideal
Google Business Profile API

Read and reply to reviews from your own system. Needs Google approval and quota. ⚠

Do not only ask happy customers for reviews. Google forbids that (“review gating”). The feedback demo sends unhappy customers to a manager but still lets anyone review.

AI and the platform

AI providers

The model that writes the replies.

Easy
OpenAI GPT-4o through S10U (stated on its site)

Already part of the platform, so nothing extra to set up.

Ideal
A private or fine-tuned model (S10U lists both)

For health, finance or children’s data. Ask how it works and where chat text is processed.

Ideal
Other providers (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude)

Reachable from South Africa and billed in USD. Ask S10U whether you can bring your own key. ⚠

Sending chat text to an overseas AI model is a cross-border transfer under POPIA. Tell customers and use a data processing agreement.

Used in:All use cases

MCP (Model Context Protocol)

A standard way for an AI to use your tools, such as “read this calendar”.

Easy
Start without it

S10U’s API, webhooks and 80+ integrations are the route to try first. S10U does not mention MCP.

Ideal
Tools that offer MCP servers

For example Zapier, HubSpot, Xero, Stripe, Shopify and Google Workspace. Confirm current status before promising it. ⚠

Ideal
Ask S10U whether MCP is planned

Then AI Studio could call your tools directly.

Privacy

POPIA (South Africa’s privacy law)

Rules for collecting and using personal information in chat.

Easy
A short privacy notice in the first chat, plus a STOP option

Say why you collect data, and let people opt out.

Ideal
Operator agreements with S10U, the AI provider and any automation tool

You are the responsible party. They are operators.

Ideal
Extra care for health, money and children’s data

Collect booking details only. Verify the parent or patient before sharing anything.

Ideal
Legal sign-off

For medical, financial and school customers.

This is a checklist, not legal advice.
